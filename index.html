About Me

My name is Julian Lavelle. I'm an independent researcher & developer in math, physics, ML, and more, working mainly with C# and C++. I write open-source libraries for the .NET ecosystem and work with low-level electronics in C++. My stack typically includes Lazulite for my C# scientific work and ESP-IDF for my low-level work.

As a developer, I tend to prioritize clean and general designs that can handle every case thrown at them. I also work towards creating solutions that are not only specific to their purpose, but also useful and innovative in their context. Growth is important to me, so I build all my projects with capacity to be scaled upwards and to be built upon in other codebases.

As a researcher, I work with physics and applied math every day. I read books on those subjects quite often (currently reading: Forest Ray Moulton's "An Introduction to Celestial Mechanics") and use the knowledge in my libraries and projects. At the moment, I'm writing a book teaching theoretical physics & replicating the Theory of Relativity from a base not derived from differential geometry.

Outside of my work, I'm a 16 year old boy in my junior year of high school. I enjoy music (in fact, I wrote a personal rythmn game to play my favorite songs!), Netflix, writing fiction, and fencing (I'm E-rated right now). I live on the US East Coast, so my timezone is EST (or UTC-4). You can reach me through my Discord at lavellex86 or my email at lavellex86@outlook.com.
